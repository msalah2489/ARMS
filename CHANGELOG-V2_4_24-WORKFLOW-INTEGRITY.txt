ARMS V2.4.24 — Workflow Integrity / Manual Receipts / IT & Supervisor Visibility

- Manual customer receipt numbers are entered from the branch paper receipt book; ARMS never generates them.
- Receipt numbers are validated as unique and one receipt number cannot be attached to multiple devices.
- Device serial numbers and non-empty device codes are protected against duplicates where the database can enforce the constraint; application validation also blocks new duplicates.
- Entering an existing serial in the branch device form automatically retrieves the registered device data (model, brand, type, color, accessories) and prevents creation of another device with that serial.
- Branch shipping availability now excludes devices already assigned to an active shipping batch. Server-side validation also prevents duplicate active-batch assignment.
- Managers can edit carrier/shipment number and exclude devices from a branch shipping batch while it is still waiting for branch handover; they can delete such a draft batch. After handover, the batch is locked.
- A branch cannot change a device route after it is tied to shipping; it must first be excluded from the batch.
- After branch handover to the carrier, the device becomes: CurrentStatus = "مرسل إلى مركز الصيانة" and CurrentLocation = "في الطريق إلى الصيانة".
- After shipping, branch request edit/cancel/customer-delivery paths remain blocked.
- Maintenance supervisor can view shipping batches, direct-customer flow, warehouse receiving status, and warehouse-arrived devices without receiving/creating/editing shipping records.
- IT/System Administrator role retains the highest administrative role and is surfaced across operational navigation.
- No EF migrations are used; schema changes are applied with startup SQL compatibility checks.
